# Reporting boundaries

The evidence behind the project's initial finding: NEXT's disclosed sourcing figures are not a comparable series. Each row records what the company stated for a reporting year, what it means for the analysis, and whether the year can be compared with the one before it.

Source IDs refer to the [published source register](Source_Register.md). The page and section locators below identify the company disclosures underlying each observation. Differences in measurement, brand scope and tier coverage prevent a like-for-like country-share trend; they do not prove that all underlying changes were caused by reporting scope.

## FY2022

### Reporting period

**Reported.** Financial year ending 29 January 2022.

**Analytical implication.** Treat the sourcing disclosure as FY2021/22 rather than calendar-year 2022 data.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year in project time series.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2022 · Page 1

### Country share measurement basis

**Reported.** Country percentages represent business volume. The ten disclosed countries produced 97% of NEXT-brand products by contracted units.

**Analytical implication.** Treat reported country shares as contracted-unit/volume shares, not procurement value by cost. Direct comparison with years using a cost-value basis would not be valid without adjustment or separate treatment.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year. Use this measurement basis as the reference when testing 2023.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Page 16

### Sourcing-map brand scope

**Reported.** The sourcing-map narrative states that NEXT sourced NEXT-brand products from 41 countries during 2021/22.

**Analytical implication.** The 2022 sourcing percentages appear to relate specifically to NEXT-brand products rather than automatically representing the full NEXT plc Group. This is more closely aligned with the NEXT-brand research scope, although product-category scope remains broader than apparel.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year. Compare later years carefully for additions of other Group brands.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Page 16

### Overall CRR reporting scope

**Reported.** The CRR covers NEXT including all NEXT plc Group companies, with its main focus on the UK and Eire

**Analytical implication.** Do not assume every metric in the CRR has the same Group-wide boundary. The sourcing map has its own more specific NEXT-brand wording, so metric-specific disclosure takes precedence.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Page 1

### Sourcing geographic coverage

**Reported.** NEXT-brand products were sourced from 41 countries; the ten disclosed sourcing countries represented 97% of products by contracted units.

**Analytical implication.** The displayed map is not the complete country distribution. The undisclosed 31 countries collectively account for approximately 3% of contracted units, so exact full-country HHI cannot be calculated from the map alone.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year. Track both total sourcing-country count and top-ten coverage in subsequent years.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Page 16

### Factory-count supplier-tier scope

**Reported.** The sourcing map reports factory counts by country, but the map page does not explicitly identify whether these counts represent Tier 1 only or a broader Tier 1/Tier 2 population. Elsewhere the CRR separately reports Tier 1 and Tier 2 supply-chain information

**Analytical implication.** Do not assume the 2022 map factory counts are directly comparable with later maps whose tier coverage is explicitly defined. Mark factory-count trend comparisons as Needs Review until tier scope is confirmed.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year; supplier-tier definition requires confirmation before later-year comparison.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Pages 14 and 16

### Product-category scope

**Reported.** The sourcing disclosure refers to NEXT-brand products and does not provide separate country shares for apparel, knitted apparel, woven apparel, homeware or individual HS categories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2022 sourcing disclosure cannot establish country concentration specifically for the selected apparel categories. It can be used as a NEXT-brand corporate sourcing baseline only.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2022 · Page 16

### General restatement policy

**Reported.** NEXT states that prior-year figures may be restated using the latest available data to improve comparability, with specific restatements identified; changes greater than 3% are considered for restatement.

**Analytical implication.** When comparing historical disclosures, use the figures actually published/restated in each relevant source and check for explicit restatement notes rather than assuming archived figures are immutable.

**Boundary change.** N/A — baseline year; use as a methodological check for subsequent reports

**Source.** NXT_RP_2022 · Page 1

## FY2023

### Reporting period

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** Corporate Responsibility Report to FY 22/23 ending on 28th January 2023.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing disclosure represents FY2022/23 and is temporally comparable with the preceding annual reporting period, subject to consistency of metric definitions and scope.

**Boundary change.** Normal annual progression from FY2021/22 to FY2022/23; no structural reporting-period change identified.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2023 · Page 1

### Country share measurement basis

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** Country percentages represent business volume; the map also reports the number of factories per country.

**Analytical implication.** The country-share metric is volume-based rather than cost-based. It appears comparable with the 2022 sourcing-share metric, but should not be combined directly with later disclosures that use sourcing value by cost.

**Boundary change.** No material change identified in the country-share measurement basis versus 2022; both are reported on a business-volume basis.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 6

### Sourcing geographic coverage

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** In FY2022/23, NEXT-branded products were sourced from 42 countries, one more than the previous year, with the top ten sourcing countries comprising 95% of total business volume.

**Analytical implication.** The disclosed top ten cover a smaller share of total sourcing than in 2022, increasing the residual share represented by countries outside the top ten from 3% to 5%.

**Boundary change.** Geographic coverage increased from 41 to 42 sourcing countries, while top-ten coverage fell from 97% to 95%.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 6

### Factory-count supplier-tier scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Needs Review***

**Reported.** The sourcing-map wording reviewed does not clearly confirm the supplier-tier population represented by the factory counts.

**Analytical implication.** Factory-count movements between 2022 and 2023 should not be interpreted as changes in NEXT's supplier footprint until the supplier-tier scope is confirmed.

**Boundary change.** Supplier-tier scope requires confirmation from the CRR and/or Reporting Principles before comparing factory counts year on year.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 6

### Sourcing-map brand scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing map reports sourcing countries for NEXT-branded products.

**Analytical implication.** The map appears to retain the NEXT-branded scope used in 2022, supporting year-on-year comparison of the country-share metric, subject to consistency of the measurement basis.

**Boundary change.** No material brand-scope change identified versus 2022.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 6

### Product-category scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing disclosure refers to NEXT-branded products and does not separately disclose country shares for apparel, knitted apparel, woven apparel, homeware or individual HS categories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2023 sourcing map cannot establish country concentration specifically for the apparel category eventually selected for this project. It provides a broader NEXT-branded sourcing baseline only.

**Boundary change.** No material product-category boundary change identified versus 2022; both disclosures remain broader than the project's eventual apparel/HS-category scope.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 6

### Overall CRR reporting scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The CRR covers NEXT including all NEXT plc Group companies, with its main focus on the UK and Eire.

**Analytical implication.** The overall CRR boundary may be broader than the sourcing-map boundary. Metric-specific sourcing-map wording should therefore take precedence when interpreting country sourcing shares.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2023 · Page 1

### General restatement policy

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT increased the restatement threshold from 3% to greater than 5% following market benchmarking. Restatements apply to identified misstatements, changes in calculation methodology, or replacement of estimated data with actual data. NEXT stated that the threshold change itself had no implications for restatements.

**Analytical implication.** Comparisons with previously reported figures should check for explicitly disclosed restatements. The change in threshold from 3% to >5% is a methodology change, but NEXT states that it did not itself result in restatement implications.

**Boundary change.** Restatement threshold increased from 3% to >5% in 2023 following market benchmarking; no restatement implications arose from the threshold change itself.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2023 · Page 1

## FY2024

### Reporting period

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** Corporate Responsibility Report to FY 23/24 ending on 27th January 2024.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing disclosure represents FY2023/24. The reporting-period structure remains comparable with FY2022/23; any break in comparability arises from changes in metric definitions or scope rather than the annual reporting period.

**Boundary change.** Normal annual progression from FY2022/23 to FY2023/24; no structural reporting-period change identified.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2024 · Page 1

### Country share measurement basis

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** Country percentages represent the percentage of the value of NEXT's business; the map also reports factory counts by country.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing-share metric changes from the volume-based measure used in 2022–2023 to a value-based measure in 2024. This creates a structural break in the sourcing time series, so 2024 country shares should not be directly compared with 2023 as evidence of sourcing-mix change.

**Boundary change.** Measurement basis changed from business volume in 2023 to business value in 2024.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2024 · Page 7

### Sourcing geographic coverage

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** In FY2023/24, NEXT-branded products were sourced from 39 countries. The ten disclosed country shares sum to 96% of reported business value.

**Analytical implication.** The number of NEXT-brand sourcing countries is comparable with the preceding year and declined from 42 to 39. However, the top-ten share of 96% in 2024 should not be directly compared with the 95% reported in 2023 because the underlying measurement basis changed from business volume to business value.

**Boundary change.** NEXT-brand sourcing-country coverage decreased from 42 to 39 countries. The 2024 top-ten shares sum to 96% of business value, but this percentage is not like-for-like with the 2023 top-ten share of 95% of business volume.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2024 · Page 7

### Factory-count supplier-tier scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Needs Review***

**Reported.** The sourcing-map wording reviewed does not clearly confirm the supplier-tier population represented by the factory counts.

**Analytical implication.** Factory-count movements between 2023 and 2024 should not be interpreted as changes in NEXT's supplier footprint until the supplier-tier scope is confirmed.

**Boundary change.** Supplier-tier scope requires confirmation from the CRR and/or Reporting Principles before comparing factory counts year on year.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2024 · Page 7

### Sourcing-map brand scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing map reports sourcing countries for NEXT-branded products.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing map continues to relate to NEXT-branded products, indicating no material change in the map's brand scope versus 2023. The separate change from business volume to business value affects country-share comparability but does not itself change the brand boundary.

**Boundary change.** No material brand-scope change identified versus 2023; the country-share measurement basis changed separately.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2024 · Page 7

### Product-category scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing disclosure refers to NEXT-branded products and does not separately disclose country shares for apparel, knitted apparel, woven apparel, homeware or individual HS categories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2024 sourcing map cannot establish country concentration specifically for the apparel category eventually selected for this project. It provides a broader NEXT-branded sourcing baseline only.

**Boundary change.** No material product-category boundary change identified versus 2023; both disclosures remain broader than the project's eventual apparel/HS-category scope.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2024 · Page 7

### Overall CRR reporting scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** NEXT is defined as NEXT plc and its subsidiaries, including minority-, majority- and wholly-owned subsidiaries. For FY24, Reiss and FatFace are excluded because NEXT had not completed a full reporting year post-acquisition; they are intended to be included in the following reporting cycle.

**Analytical implication.** The overall CRR organisational boundary includes explicit acquisition-related exclusions in FY24. This broader CRR boundary should not be assumed to define the sourcing map, whose metric-specific wording continues to refer to NEXT-branded products. The sourcing-map boundary therefore takes precedence for country-share analysis.

**Boundary change.** FY24 introduces explicit exclusions for Reiss and FatFace from the overall CR reporting boundary pending a full post-acquisition reporting year.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2024 · Page 1

### General restatement policy

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT considers restatement of previously reported data necessary where there is a change greater than 5%. Where restatements are made for specific indicators, they are clearly outlined.

**Analytical implication.** Historical comparisons should be checked for explicitly disclosed restatements before previously published values are treated as unchanged. The restatement threshold itself remains consistent with the >5% threshold introduced in 2023.

**Boundary change.** No material change versus 2023; the restatement threshold remains greater than 5%.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2024 · Page 2

## FY2025

### Reporting period

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** Corporate Responsibility reporting covers the 52 weeks ended 25 January 2025.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing disclosure represents FY2024/25. The reporting-period length remains consistent with FY2023/24, so no temporal break is introduced by the annual period itself.

**Boundary change.** No structural reporting-period change versus FY2023/24; both reporting periods contain 52 weeks.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2025 · Page 1

### Country share measurement basis

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** Country percentages represent the percentage of the value of the business; the map also reports the number of factories per country.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing-share metric remains value-based, consistent with the measurement wording used in 2024. However, year-on-year country-share comparability still depends on the brand and supplier-tier scope of the map.

**Boundary change.** No material change identified in the stated measurement basis versus 2024; both years report country shares as percentage of business value.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Sourcing geographic coverage

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** In FY2024/25, NEXT sourced NEXT-brand products from 35 countries.

**Analytical implication.** The disclosed NEXT-brand geographic footprint decreased from 39 sourcing countries in 2024 to 35 in 2025. This country-count metric is distinct from the broader brand scope of the top-ten sourcing map.

**Boundary change.** NEXT-brand sourcing-country coverage decreased from 39 countries in FY2023/24 to 35 countries in FY2024/25.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Factory-count supplier-tier scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Needs Review***

**Reported.** The 2025 sourcing map depicts Tier 1 supplier factories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2025 factory counts explicitly represent Tier 1 sites. The 2024 map did not explicitly identify the tier population represented by its country-level factory counts, so factory-count changes between 2024 and 2025 cannot yet be treated as fully like-for-like.

**Boundary change.** 2025 explicitly defines the mapped sites as Tier 1 factories; equivalent 2024 tier coverage was not explicitly stated on the map.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Sourcing-map brand scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** The map includes Tier 1 supplier factories for NEXT, wholly owned brands (Lipsy, Friends Like These and Love & Roses), FatFace and Joules. Reiss and JoJo Maman Bébé are excluded.

**Analytical implication.** The sourcing map no longer represents a NEXT-brand-only footprint. Consequently, the 2025 country value shares cannot be directly compared with the 2024 NEXT-brand sourcing shares as a like-for-like sourcing-mix trend.

**Boundary change.** Material scope expansion versus 2024: the map moves from a NEXT-brand sourcing disclosure to a mixed-brand disclosure including wholly owned brands, FatFace and Joules.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Product-category scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing map does not separately disclose country shares for apparel, knitted apparel, woven apparel, homeware or individual HS categories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2025 map cannot establish geographic concentration for the project's eventual apparel or HS-category scope. It provides a broader product sourcing baseline only.

**Boundary change.** No material product-category disclosure improvement versus 2024; country shares remain broader than the project's eventual apparel/HS-category scope.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Supply-chain tier structure

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT describes its product supply chain from raw materials to final products as comprising five tiers.

**Analytical implication.** The Tier 1 sourcing map represents only one level of the broader five-tier supply chain and should not be interpreted as complete upstream sourcing exposure.

**Boundary change.** No material change identified in NEXT's five-tier supply-chain framework versus 2024.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2025 · Page 10

### Overall CRR reporting scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** NEXT is defined as the legal entity NEXT plc and its subsidiaries, including minority-, majority- and wholly-owned subsidiaries. Acquired subsidiaries are incorporated into relevant reporting when reliable and accurate data is available, with NEXT seeking inclusion in the first full reporting period after acquisition.

**Analytical implication.** The overall CRR organisational boundary is broader than the sourcing-map boundary and should not be used as a substitute for the map's metric-specific scope. Compared with FY2024, the explicit exclusion of Reiss and FatFace from the general reporting boundary is no longer stated.

**Boundary change.** The FY2024 Reporting Principles explicitly excluded Reiss and FatFace pending a full post-acquisition reporting year; that explicit exclusion is removed in FY2025, indicating expansion of the general organisational reporting boundary.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2025 · Page 1

### General restatement policy

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT considers restatement necessary where previously reported data changes by greater than 5%. Restatements may result from structural changes to reporting scope, significant methodology changes or improvements in data accuracy, and significant errors or cumulative errors.

**Analytical implication.** Historical figures should be checked for explicit restatements before being treated as unchanged. NEXT also states that structural reporting changes may require recalculation of baselines or other historical data to maintain consistency.

**Boundary change.** The >5% materiality threshold remains unchanged from 2024. The 2025 principles provide additional explicit detail on structural changes, methodology changes and corrections.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2025 · Page 1-2

## FY2026

### Reporting period

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** Corporate Responsibility reporting covers the 53 weeks ended 31 January 2026.

**Analytical implication.** FY2025/26 contains an additional 53rd week. The reporting period is therefore not strictly identical in length to FY2024/25 and this should be annotated when comparing annual sourcing observations.

**Boundary change.** Reporting period increased from 52 weeks in FY2024/25 to 53 weeks in FY2025/26.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2026 · Page 1

### Country share measurement basis

*Comparable with the previous year: **Needs Review***

**Reported.** Country percentages represent the percentage of business value measured by cost; the map also reports the number of factories per country.

**Analytical implication.** The 2026 disclosure is explicitly cost-value based. The 2025 map was also value-based but did not explicitly state “by cost”, so identical measurement methodology should not be assumed solely from wording.

**Boundary change.** Both years are value-based, but 2026 explicitly clarifies the measure as value by cost. No like-for-like country-share trend should be inferred without confirming that the 2025 value measure used the same cost basis.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Sourcing geographic coverage

*Comparable with the previous year: **Needs Review***

**Reported.** In FY2025/26, NEXT-branded products were sourced from Tier 1 and Tier 2 factories in 33 countries.

**Analytical implication.** The reported country count falls from 35 in 2025 to 33 in 2026, but the 2026 disclosure explicitly defines the count through Tier 1 and Tier 2 factories whereas the 2025 geographic statement was not expressed with the same tier qualification.

**Boundary change.** Reported geographic coverage decreases from 35 to 33 countries, but the 2026 wording introduces an explicit Tier 1 and Tier 2 boundary; treat the apparent decline cautiously.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Factory-count supplier-tier scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **No***

**Reported.** The map includes Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier factories for NEXT and wholly owned brands, and Tier 1 supplier factories for FatFace and Joules.

**Analytical implication.** The 2026 factory population is broader than the 2025 map, which included Tier 1 supplier factories only. Both country-level site counts and country-value shares may therefore be affected by the changed tier population.

**Boundary change.** Material tier-scope expansion: 2025 map = Tier 1 only; 2026 adds Tier 2 for NEXT and wholly owned brands while FatFace and Joules remain Tier 1.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Sourcing-map brand scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The map includes NEXT and wholly owned brands, plus FatFace and Joules; Reiss and JoJo Maman Bébé are excluded.

**Analytical implication.** The principal brand population appears materially consistent with the 2025 sourcing map. The major 2026 comparability problem arises from supplier-tier scope rather than the named brand set.

**Boundary change.** No material change identified in the named sourcing-map brand population versus 2025; Reiss and JoJo Maman Bébé remain excluded.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Product-category scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** The sourcing disclosure does not separately provide country value shares for apparel, knitted apparel, woven apparel, homeware or individual HS categories.

**Analytical implication.** The 2026 map still cannot establish NEXT own-brand apparel or selected-HS-category sourcing concentration. The 73% top-three figure therefore remains a broader disclosed sourcing statistic rather than an apparel-specific procurement measure.

**Boundary change.** No material improvement in product-category granularity versus 2025.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Supply-chain tier structure

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT describes its supply chain from raw material sources to final products as comprising five tiers.

**Analytical implication.** Even though the 2026 map expands into Tier 2 for some brands, it still does not represent the complete five-tier upstream supply chain.

**Boundary change.** The five-tier conceptual supply-chain structure remains unchanged; only the sourcing-map tier coverage changes.

**Source.** NXT_CRR_2026 · Page 10

### Overall CRR reporting scope

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT is defined as the legal entity NEXT plc and its subsidiaries, including minority-, majority- and wholly-owned subsidiaries. Acquired subsidiaries are incorporated into relevant reporting when reliable and accurate information becomes available, with NEXT seeking inclusion in the first full reporting period following acquisition.

**Analytical implication.** The general organisational reporting framework remains broader than any individual sourcing-map metric. Metric-specific sourcing-map wording must therefore continue to take precedence for country-share interpretation.

**Boundary change.** No material change identified in the general organisational-boundary framework versus 2025.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2026 · Page 1

### General restatement policy

*Comparable with the previous year: **Yes***

**Reported.** NEXT considers restatement necessary where previously reported data changes by greater than 5%. Restatement may arise from structural reporting changes, significant methodology changes or improvements in data accuracy, and significant individual or cumulative errors.

**Analytical implication.** Historical figures should continue to be checked for explicit restatements. Structural or methodological changes can trigger recalculation of historical baselines or related data to preserve comparability.

**Boundary change.** No material change versus 2025; the >5% threshold and structural/methodological/correction framework are retained.

**Source.** NXT_RP_2026 · Page 1-2
